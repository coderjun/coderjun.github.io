About

Jun Heider is a streaming media architect with 15+ years of experience building video infrastructure for products at scale. He's worked across the full stack—transcoding pipelines, CDN optimization, live delivery, and playback performance—for companies ranging from startups to Peacock and Dolby.

Jun is the founder of Ajnunna Systems, a consultancy helping product teams build and scale streaming systems without hiring a full video engineering team. He also teaches "AI for the Streaming SDLC" at SVTA University.
